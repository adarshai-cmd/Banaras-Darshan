## 1. Licensing & Usage Policy

All photography featured in the seed database and default UI layouts is sourced under open-access creative licenses:
- **Unsplash License:** Granted for free commercial and non-commercial usage without mandatory permission, provided photographs are not compiled to replicate a competing photo-aggregation service.
- **Wikimedia Commons / Creative Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0 / CC BY 2.0):** Used for architectural and religious heritage documentation where indicated.
- **Spiritual Photography Policy:** In accordance with Kashi Vishwanath and Manikarnika Ghat customs, photography is strictly prohibited inside the inner sanctorum (*Garbhagriha*) of temples and at the active cremation platforms of Manikarnika and Harishchandra Ghats. Images presented in Banaras Darshan depict exterior corridors, architectural facade highlights, morning river vistas, or public riverfront sweeps taken from approved ghat landings.

## 3. Contribution & Submission Guidelines

When submitting new places via the user profile or staff moderation panel:
1. Provide a direct, high-resolution photo URL from verified domains (`images.unsplash.com`, `upload.wikimedia.org`, or official tourism portals).
2. Ensure the photo accurately reflects the physical site and architectural character of Varanasi.
3. Obey religious sanctum photography bans and respect citizen privacy in sacred spaces.
